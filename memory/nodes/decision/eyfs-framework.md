**Decided:** 2026-10-02.

**Why:** EYFS is the framework English settings use from birth to 5, so it gives a shared
language with any nursery, health visitor or later school, and Development Matters gives
age-banded observations to plan against. Home educators are not required to follow it.

**Consequence:** Communication and language, Physical development and PSED get most activities
until about age 3; the four specific areas grow from there.
