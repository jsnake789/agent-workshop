# Seasonal calendar, West Midlands (rough guide)

Adjust to the year's weather. First frosts usually October, last frosts late April or May.

| Month | Land | Dogs | With Willow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan | Check fences and gates after storms; clear drains; plan the year | Muddy paws routine; check for cuts on frozen ground | Frost and ice exploring, bird feeders |
| Feb | Finish hedge cutting before 1 March; order seeds | Book annual boosters if due | Watching birds at feeders |
| Mar | Hedge cutting stops (nesting season to 31 Aug); first mow if dry | Tick season starts, review treatment | Spotting buds and lambs nearby |
| Apr | Sow hardy seeds; check for ground-nesting birds before mowing | Ticks: check after walks | Planting seeds in pots |
| May | Mow paths, leave some long grass for wildlife | Watch for grass seeds in paws and ears | Bugs, flowers, textures |
| Jun | Water new plantings; ragwort check if grazing | Heat: walk early, shade, water | Water play outside |
| Jul | Top-up mowing; harvest | Heat and blue-green algae warnings near ponds | Picking and tasting |
| Aug | Hay or long grass cut late; prep for autumn | Grass seeds; heat | Collecting and sorting |
| Sep | Hedge cutting allowed again; sow green manure | Booster and worming check | Apples, blackberries |
| Oct | Leaf collecting; clear gutters; protect taps | Darker walks: hi-vis and lights; conkers and acorns are toxic | Leaf piles, conkers (supervised) |
| Nov | Plant bare-root hedging and trees; firework prep | Fireworks: safe space, keep indoors | Puddles, mud, wellies |
| Dec | Check shelter and drainage; grit paths | Christmas food hazards (chocolate, grapes, raisins) | Lights, textures, winter walks |

Hedge-cutting dates reflect common UK guidance (and farm rules for those claiming
payments); check current rules if they apply.
