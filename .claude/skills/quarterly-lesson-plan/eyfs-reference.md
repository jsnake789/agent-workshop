# EYFS quick reference

Statutory framework: *Early years foundation stage statutory framework* (DfE).
Planning guidance (non-statutory): *Development Matters* (DfE) and *Birth to 5 Matters*.
Home educators in England are not required to follow EYFS; it is used here as a shared
language and a sensible progression.

## Areas of learning

**Prime** (the foundation, emphasised for babies and toddlers)
- Communication and language
- Physical development
- Personal, social and emotional development

**Specific** (grow from the prime areas)
- Literacy
- Mathematics
- Understanding the world
- Expressive arts and design

## Characteristics of effective teaching and learning

- Playing and exploring: finding out, having a go.
- Active learning: concentrating, keeping on trying, enjoying achievement.
- Creating and thinking critically: own ideas, making links, choosing ways to do things.

## Rough age bands used for planning

Development Matters groups observations as birth to 3, 3 and 4 year olds, and Reception.
Within birth to 3 we plan in 3-month steps (7 to 9 months, 10 to 12, 13 to 15, ...)
because babies change so fast. Treat any age guidance as typical ranges, not targets.

## Example observable goals, 7 to 12 months (illustrative)

- C&L: turns to own name; babbles with consonants; responds to familiar words.
- PD: sits unsupported; begins to crawl or bottom-shuffle; passes objects hand to hand;
  pincer grasp emerging.
- PSED: seeks familiar adults for comfort; shows wariness of strangers; enjoys
  turn-taking games like peekaboo.
- UW: explores objects with hands and mouth; watches animals and moving things outdoors.
- EAD: responds to music with movement; bangs objects to make sounds.

Verify current wording against the latest Development Matters before quoting it.
